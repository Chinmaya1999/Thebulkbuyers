FAQ

Good questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is set per style by each manufacturer — typically 200–600 pieces. You can see it on every product card.

Does it cost anything to post an RFQ?

No. Posting RFQs and comparing quotes is free for buyers. The platform charges the seller a small commission on confirmed orders.

Can I get custom labels and my own designs?

Yes. Mention private label, prints, embroidery or packaging in your RFQ notes and sellers will quote accordingly.

How are sellers verified?

New sellers register and wait for admin verification before they can quote. Products are reviewed before going live.

Do you ship internationally?

Quotes are FOB India by default; specify your destination port or city in the RFQ and sellers will include freight options.
